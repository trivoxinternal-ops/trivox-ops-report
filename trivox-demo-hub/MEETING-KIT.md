# MEETING KIT — Construction AI Demo (Live)

**Audience:** Construction company hiring an AI Automation Engineer (first technical hire).
**They care about:** AI estimating pipelines, accounting integrations, real-time dashboards.
**Your job:** Show outcomes and speed. Make them feel the time and money they're leaving on the table today.

---

## 0. Setup (do this before they walk in)

- Open `trivox-demo-hub/index.html` in your browser (Chrome recommended).
- Press **F11** (or Cmd+Ctrl+F) for **full screen** — kill tabs, bookmarks, notifications.
- **Do NOT rely on venue wifi.** Every AI demo runs **fully offline** — load the hub once and you're safe.
- **Only exception:** the **Cognizance Report Engine** card needs internet. If wifi is flaky, tether to your phone before that one, or skip it and circle back.
- Close Slack/email/DND on. Zoom the browser to ~110% so the room can read the screen.

---

## 1. The 60-Second Opening

> "Thanks for having me. Before I show you anything, here's the frame: every demo you're about to see takes seconds, runs on this laptop, and replaces hours of someone's week. I'm not here to talk about models or pipelines — I'm here to show you the output your team would actually ship. We'll start where your money is made and lost — estimating — then walk through accounting, the dashboards your PMs would live in, and the field and phone work that quietly eats your margins. Scope in, money out. Let me show you."

Then go straight into Demo 1. Don't linger.

---

## 2. Demo Order + Talk Track (one line each — OUTCOME, never tech)

Run in this order to mirror their priorities: **Estimating → Accounting → Dashboards → Field/Comms.**

### ESTIMATING (open strong — this is their P&L)
1. **AI Estimate & Quote Generator** — "Paste the scope, and you hand the client a branded, itemized quote before they've finished their coffee."
2. **AI Material Takeoff & Cost** — "Drop in dimensions and walk away with a supplier-ready material order — no spreadsheet, no missed line item."
3. **AI Bid / RFP Analyzer** — "Paste any RFP and instantly see the scope, the hidden risks, and a defensible bid range before a competitor's even read it."
4. **Employee Cost Calculator** — "Know your true, fully-loaded labor cost per role on the spot — so you never bid a job underwater again."

### ACCOUNTING & FINANCE
5. **AI Accounting & Invoicing Hub** — "Invoices, AR/AP, and reconciliation run themselves, so you stop chasing money and start collecting it faster."
6. **Change Order & Approval Flow** — "One change order, instant cost and schedule impact, one-click approval, and a live revised contract total — no more margin leaking through scope creep."

### DASHBOARDS / OPERATIONS
7. **Real-Time Project Command Center** — "Every active project's budget burn, schedule risk, and health in one live view — so problems surface days before they cost you."
8. **Report Engine** — "Branded operational reports in one click — the Monday-morning rollup that used to eat an analyst's afternoon."
9. **Cognizance Report Engine** *(needs internet)* — "Polished client-ready reports generated in seconds — your clients think you hired a whole reporting team."

### FIELD & COMMUNICATION
10. **AI Daily Site Report** — "Rough field notes go in, a professional daily log comes out — your foremen document the whole site in two minutes from the truck."
11. **Construction AI Voice Agent** — "It answers every call, qualifies the lead, and books the site visit 24/7 — you stop losing jobs to voicemail."

### LIVE CASE STUDY (proof, close with this)
12. **Campos Contracting** — "And this isn't theory — here's a real, custom build running live for a contractor just like you."

---

## 3. THE GOLDEN RULE

**Show the OUTCOME and the SPEED. Never reveal the build.**

- No code. No n8n. No prompts. No screens behind the curtain.
- They should remember *the quote that appeared in 4 seconds* — not *how* it appeared.
- If something loads slowly, talk over it about the outcome. Never narrate the mechanics.
- Every demo ends with a number or a saved hour: "That just saved your estimator half a day."

---

## 4. Answering "How does it work?" (without giving away the magic)

1. "It's a layered automation system we design and tune specifically for your workflows — the engineering lives under the hood so your team only ever touches the clean output."
2. "Think of it like hiring a specialist who never sleeps: you feed it the same inputs your team already has, and it returns finished, on-brand work in seconds."
3. "The 'how' is exactly what this role builds and owns — today I'm showing you what's possible, and in the build phase we go deep on the pipeline together."
